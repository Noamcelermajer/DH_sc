# Rights and provenance

This is an independent reconstruction of selected Dungeon Hunter II engine
behavior from supplied application binaries and game data. It is not an
official source release and does not include the original development source.

The game, its assets, binaries, and recovered material remain subject to their
respective rights holders. This repository does not grant a license to those
materials or assert a repository-wide open-source license. Review the notices
distributed with each third-party component before using or redistributing it.

The port modules contain independently written implementations and interfaces
based on observed binary behavior. Their inclusion does not imply access to or
recovery of the original studio source. Provenance and input requirements are
documented with each module.
